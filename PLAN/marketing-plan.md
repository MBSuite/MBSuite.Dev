# Marketing Plan for MBSuite

## 1. Vision & Positioning
- **Vision:** Empower Thai SMEs (F&B, Retail, Service, Rental, Pharmacy, Hotel) with an integrated, affordable accounting & operations suite.
- **Tagline:** "All‑in‑One Business Suite – Simple, Transparent, Scalable."
- **Unique Selling Points:**
  1. Flexible pricing (monthly, yearly, perpetual) tailored to cash‑flow needs.
  2. Modular architecture – pick only the modules you need.
  3. Localized (Thai language, ICT timezone) + Thai‑specific tax & compliance.
  4. No vendor lock‑in – perpetual licence option.

## 2. Target Personas
| Persona | Business Type | Pain Points | Desired Outcome |
|---------|---------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **พ.1** – เจ้าของร้านอาหาร/คาเฟ่ (10‑30 staff) | F&B | Manual sales & inventory tracking, messy receipts, limited staff tech skills. | Simple QR ordering, real‑time sales dashboard, easy tax filing. |
| **พ.2** – ผู้จัดการทรัพย์สินเช่า (คอนโด/อพาร์ทเมนท์) | Rental | Multiple units, rent‑payment tracking, late fees, accounting sync. | Centralised rent ledger, automatic reminders, integration with Core accounting. |
| **พ.3** – เจ้าของร้านค้าปลีกขนาดเล็ก | Retail | Stock‑out, price‑list errors, manual cash‑closing. | Barcode‑enabled POS, low‑stock alerts, daily closing reports. |
| **พ.4** – ผู้ให้บริการ (ซัก‑รีด, ทำความสะอาด) | Service | Job scheduling, contract renewals, invoicing. | Calendar‑driven appointments, contract‑renewal alerts, auto‑invoice generation. |
| **พ.5** – ผู้บริหารระดับสูง (CFO/Owner) | All modules | Consolidated financial view, ROI awareness, compliance. | One‑click P&L, cash‑flow forecast, export to Thai tax portal. |

## 3. Acquisition Channels & Tactics
| Channel | Tactic | Budget (THB) | KPI |
|--------|--------|--------------|-----|
| **Content Marketing** | Weekly blog posts on Thai tax tips, inventory best‑practices, case studies. SEO‑optimized (target keywords: "ซอฟต์แวร์บัญชีฟรี", "ระบบ POS ร้านอาหาร"). | 30,000 | Reach 10k unique readers / month, 3% click‑through to sign‑up. |
| **Social Media** | Facebook Groups (Thai SME community), LINE OA broadcast, TikTok short demos (30‑sec). Use micro‑influencers (SME consultants). | 20,000 | 5,000 followers, 200 demo clicks / month. |
| **Paid Ads** | Google Search (keywords above), Facebook Lead Ads (Free 14‑day trial). Start with 15,000 THB/month, optimise CPA. | 15,000 | CPA ≤ 150 THB, 300 trial sign‑ups / month. |
| **Partnerships** | Accountant & bookkeeping firms – co‑marketing webinars, referral commission 10% of first‑year subscription. | 0 (partner‑shared) | 5 partner referrals / month. |
| **Community & Events** | Sponsor local SME meet‑ups, host live Q&A webinars (Thai tax season). | 10,000 | 150 attendees, 10% conversion to trial. |
| **Email Nurture** | Drip sequence (welcome → ROI calculator → case study → trial reminder). Use Resend integration for transactional & marketing emails. | 5,000 (Resend) | Open rate 45%, conversion 8% from drip. |
| **Referral Program** | “Invite a friend, get 1 month free” – track via unique referral codes. | 5,000 (discount budget) | 50 new trials via referrals / month. |

## 4. Conversion Funnel
1. **Awareness** – Blog / Social / Ads → Landing page (hero with CTA “ลองฟรี 14 วัน”).
2. **Interest** – Landing page → Short demo video → ROI calculator (embedded widget). Capture email before calculator.
3. **Consideration** – Email drip + case studies + live demo booking (Calendly integration).
4. **Decision** – Pricing comparison table (Monthly / Yearly / Perpetual) → Clear CTA “สมัครเดี๋ยวนี้” (Subscribe) or “ซื้อโดยตรง”.
5. **Retention** – Onboarding guide, in‑app tutorials, quarterly webinars, upsell to additional modules.

### Copy / CTA Recommendations
- Hero headline: **“จัดการธุรกิจของคุณในหนึ่งแพลตฟอร์ม – ง่าย ไม่ซับซ้อน”**
- Sub‑headline: **“ทดลองฟรี 14 วัน – ไม่มีบัตรเครดิต”**
- Button text: **“เริ่มทดลองฟรี”** → leads to sign‑up page.
- Pricing section tagline: **“เลือกตามความต้องการของคุณ – รายเดือน, รายปี, หรือซื้อเอง”**
- Trust signals: Logos of partner accountants, Thai tax office endorsement badge.

## 7. Risks & Mitigations
- **Regulatory compliance:** Verify price disclosures match Thai consumer protection law – coordinate with finance/legal before launch.
- **Pricing confusion:** Use visual comparison table and clear badge for “Perpetual – No recurring fees”.
- **Channel overload:** Prioritise low‑cost organic channels first; scale paid ads only after CPA stabilises.
- **Data privacy:** Ensure email collection complies with PDPA (Thai GDPR) – add consent checkbox.
